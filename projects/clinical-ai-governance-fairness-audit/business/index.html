02 · Architecture & Control Flow

System Topology & Failure-Mode Lifecycles

End-to-end execution graph paired with nominal request flow and adversarial / degraded fault-handling paths.

flowchart LR
    DATA["Clinical Diabetes Triage Cohort"] --> BASE["Baseline Classifier\nIntersectional Subgroup Audit"]
    BASE --> SHAP["TreeSHAP Proxy Attribution\nInsurance -> Socioeconomic Bias"]
    SHAP --> DEBIAS["Counterfactual Sample Re-Weighting\n-31.4 pp Medicaid-Private FNR Gap"]
    DEBIAS --> GRC["EU AI Act Annex III + NIST AI RMF\nKRIs · MITRE ATLAS · Schrems II TIA"]

✓ Nominal Production Request Path

  1. audit.py evaluates held-out clinical cohort (N = 3,000, operating threshold 0.26) across marginal and Region x Insurance intersectional slices.
  2. Identifies Rural x Medicaid (n=284, pos=33) as most-harmed subgroup (60.6% FNR vs 23.1% Rural x Private -> +37.5 pp within-rural disparity).
  3. shap_analysis.py computes global & local TreeSHAP attributions confirming insurance_type suppresses risk scores for Medicaid patients with identical clinical vitals.
  4. Counterfactual retraining without insurance_type reduces disparity by 31.4 pp while preserving 0.807 ROC-AUC, governed continuously by kri.py thresholds.

✕ Adversarial, Policy & Fault-Handling Paths

  • Subgroups with n_positives < 20 (e.g., Rural x Uninsured, pos=10) are flagged Sample < 20 by find_most_harmed() so noisy cells never misdirect remediation.
  • Division-by-zero edge cases (cohorts with zero positives or zero negatives) return explicit NaN contracts tested in test_fairness_metrics.py.
  • Clinician override spike or unstructured override drift trips kri.py AMBER / RED governance bands, halting silent post-deployment clinical degradation.

04 · Engineering Trade-Offs (ADRs)

Why This Architecture Over Naive Prototypes?

Key architectural decisions balancing determinism, latency, cost, privacy, and operational maintainability.

Architectural Decision Naive Prototype Anti-Pattern Production Engineering Choice & Outcome
12-Cell Intersectional Audit vs. Marginal Single-Axis Fairness Checking Region alone or Insurance alone—where Urban x Medicaid (19.2% FNR) masks severe Rural x Medicaid (60.6% FNR) clinical under-triage. Multi-index Region x Insurance auditing with n_positives >= 20 statistical power gates pinpoints the exact harmed cohort.
TreeSHAP Proxy Proof + Feature Ablation vs. Blind Threshold Tweaking Lowering the global classification threshold for everyone, inflating False Positives across all clinics without fixing within-region proxy bias. Mechanistic TreeSHAP attribution + counterfactual insurance_type ablation cuts rural disparity by 31.4 pp while holding 0.807 ROC-AUC.
Dual-Metric Clinician Override KRI (Rate + Structured Share) Tracking only model prediction drift while ignoring clinicians overriding or bypassing triage alerts in production. Joint KRI gating on override volume AND structured justification share (kri.py), catching automation bias and workflow friction early.
